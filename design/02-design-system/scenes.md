Each section is built like a shot: it declares its **light**, its **subject**, the **composition** it borrows (never the imagery: nobody should name the reference at first glance), its **bars** and its **theme**. The names below are internal; on the site each section is called what it is.

## Per-section rules

- **01 · Home** — Opening shot, played once per visit (~5s, eased out): a push-in from a full-body wide of ROB-092 to a medium close-up where his head covers the base of the name. The name is part of the 3D set (gold fill, brightest top-left, lit and shadowed for real) with a visually hidden `<h1>`. The copy never moves: bottom-left the claim "Designer & engineer. One person.", one line of body, **See the projects** (primary) and **Ask the robot** (secondary, jumps to About); bottom-right the stack in a small billing block. ROB-092 is silent here; no Caveat note. The close-up is the og:image.
- **01 → 02** (scrubbed, about two screens, snapping off) — the copy fades out with a GSAP stagger · ROB-092 turns 180° so we end on his back · the camera pushes in past him and he settles into the blurred crowd at the bottom of Projects · the projects screen switches on.
- **02 · Projects** — A real rendered cinema, camera in the middle rows, **no black bars**: the nav and bottom row float over the room on soft dark gradients, so the visitor sits inside it. **The screen is only a picture:** a flat front-on rectangle on columns 1–6 (680×382 at desktop, top at 112px), playing the trailer, no UI on it, no effects. **The right column (columns 8–12, top aligned with the screen) has fixed-height blocks that never move, whatever the content:** a 28px row with `01 / 09` and the facts · 40px · a 64px one-line title in Tac One **cream** (56px; names over 12 characters drop to 44px; never wraps) · 16px · a 52px lead box (17px, two lines, clamped) · 32px · the 48px CTA pair (**View the case study** · **Next · <project> · 07s**, whose yellow underline is the trailer's timer) · 48px · the index, four 44px rows tall, scrolling vertically, fading at the bottom, a 2px track on the right. Gold stays the hero's: here the screen is the one subject, so the title is cream. **Hover a title:** the row lights (cream, yellow number, →), a 224×140 thumbnail floats up beside the cursor, tilted −3°, overlapping the screen's edge, and the screen cuts to that trailer. **Trailers:** the first plays as you arrive, with sound if sound is on; when one ends the next starts and the list follows. The video's colour lights the room and the audience. Sequence in: ROB-092 turns his back · the spotlight dies · 3 black frames · the screen ignites as a line, then the picture. Opening a project grows the screen to fill the viewport and it becomes the case-study hero.
- **03 · About** (v23, full spec in the project's `About-scene.md`) — The cream wall has a thin, crisp triangular cut (no bevel) opening onto a deep tunnel that fades to pure black: nothing readable inside, no warm light. **ROB-092 leans out of the cut like someone at a window**, forearms folded on its bottom edge, looking at you; his hips stay behind the wall, so nothing clips. Triangle apex y 133, base y 717 at 1440×900, centred on its columns. **Right column (x 980, width 396):** headline "Ask me / about / Ettore." (Alumni 800, 84/0.84, ink — deliberately between `poster-l` and `poster-xl`) · +20px a context line (`body`, `dust`) · +28px the ask line (blinking ink caret, "Type your question", a yellow ASK ↵ key with ink text) · a hairline at y 606, then a 2×2 grid of Facts whose last row sits level with the cut's base. **Caption** bottom-left (x 64, y 736): ROB-092 / Public relations unit. **Bars:** the Home rows on black, "03 · About", "Contact ↓", "03 / 05". **The interview is hybrid:** suggested questions play pre-written answers; only free-typed questions reach a guarded free-tier LLM that answers only about Ettore ("An AI trained on Ettore's CV. May embellish. Nothing is stored."). Answers type on at ~40 chars/s with gibberish audio. **Motion:** idle breathing, the head tracks the cursor and looks at you while he speaks, a nod on send, hole parallax; reduced motion: static. The cut in is a 2-frame white flash. **Mobile:** the render on top between the bars, then headline, context and ask line; asking opens a full-screen interview (no bars) with the answer above the keyboard.
- **04 · Contact** — "CONTACT" in `marquee-l`, `text-loud`, `glow-red`. The email is the subject of the left column: `poster-l`, cream, `red` underline. One primary: "Copy email".
- **05 · Footer** — BillingBlock credits centred on the grid, then the name in `marquee-xl` gold, then © · back to top on one baseline, then ROB-092's last line ("Is he hired yet?").
- **Case study** — hero (loved, keep), story + EpisodeRow list, palette & type, final result, more projects + Button `timer` ("Next project in 07s").
