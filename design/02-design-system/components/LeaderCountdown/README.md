# LeaderCountdown

The loading page: a countdown whose sweep and numeral are the real loading progress.

- Tac One numeral in `yellow` with glow, two thin cream rings, a crosshair, a cream sweep at ~20% driven by load progress. 5 → 1 maps onto 0–100%: a fast connection sees a short countdown.
- **No bars at first.** From 80% load the black bars slide in from top and bottom, eased out, reaching `bar-height` at 100%: the visitor enters the site. Nothing else moves when they arrive.
- **Name and role from the first frame** (bottom-left): "Loading · 64%" with a thin progress line, ETTORE SERRA, "Designer & engineer · Turin, or remote".
- **The choice** (bottom-right): "Enter with sound" (primary) / "Enter in silence" (secondary), the browser's required gesture for audio, with one Caveat note ("best with sound on"). Loaded before the click: it holds on 1, ring full. Remember the choice.
- **Return visits** skip it: one 0.6s light-leak frame on 1, then the cut to the hero.
- Plain copy only: no reels or film words. The timecode and the sound toggle sit top-right.
