# RobotSubtitle

How ROB-092 speaks outside the About interview (e.g. Contact, the post-credits line): an untranslated Caveat line in `text-note`, then the subtitle on an 84% black box.

- **Provide:** the gibberish (`* asterisks *`, lowercase) and the subtitle text (≤ 2 lines, ~34ch). Add `.who` "ROB-092" only when the robot is off-screen.
- Subtitle is Instrument Sans `subtitle` (22/600) in `cream` — always on the dark box, even on paper.
- Types on at ~40 chars/s while gibberish audio plays; hold 2.5s after the last character.
- Position under or beside the robot, never over his face.
